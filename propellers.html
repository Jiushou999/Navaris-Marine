Propellers

New and replacement propellers for commercial vessels, supplied to your specification.

What we supply

  • New propellers made to your vessel's specification or to match an existing unit
  • Replacement propellers for worn, damaged or lost units
  • Reconditioning and repair coordination, including inspection, repair, polishing and balancing through qualified workshops
  • Technical clarification of pitch, diameter, material and fit before you order

What we need for a quotation

Vessel
Name, type and IMO number if available
Existing propeller
Diameter, pitch, number of blades, material, rotation, bore and taper
Drawing or photos
Propeller drawing, nameplate or clear photos of the existing unit
Class and documents
Class society and any certificates required
Delivery
Delivery port and the date you need it by
