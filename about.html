About us

Trusted Intelligence at Sea. A Singapore-based marine company built around technical know-how, trust and long-term performance.

Who we are

Navaris Marine Pte Ltd (恒瑞海业有限公司) supplies propellers and marine spare parts, and provides deck equipment consultancy and engineering support, including electrical solutions and vessel retrofit projects.

Our team of engineers brings more than a decade of experience working with vessel operations around the world. Their in-depth knowledge helps clients achieve their goals and go beyond.

We combine a maritime engineering background with a technology mindset: practical advice and dependable supply, with an eye on where vessels and offshore operations are heading.

What we focus on

  • Propellers: new and replacement units to specification
  • Deck equipment consultancy: winches, windlasses, cranes and related machinery
  • Engineering works: electrical solutions (full electric propulsion, hybrid systems, BMS), vessel engineering works and retrofit projects
  • Spares trading: electrical, mechanical and hydraulic spares and fittings, quoted against part numbers and drawings

Who we serve

  • Shipowners and fleet operators
  • Offshore energy and marine contractors
  • Shipyards and vessel designers
  • Ship managers, ports and marine technology providers
