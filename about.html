About us

A Singapore-based marine company built around technical know-how and straightforward service.

Who we are

Navaris Marine Pte Ltd supplies propellers and marine spare parts, and provides deck equipment consultancy and basic engineering support. We are led by an engineer with more than a decade of marine and offshore technical sales experience across Asia-Pacific.

What we focus on

  • Propellers: new and replacement units to specification
  • Deck equipment consultancy: winches, windlasses, cranes and related machinery
  • Basic engineering works: minor repairs, installation support and modifications
  • Spares trading: quoted against part numbers and drawings

How we work

We keep the process simple. You tell us what the vessel needs, we check the technical details with you, and we reply with a written quotation that states what is included, where it comes from and when it can arrive. Where a job needs a specialist, we say so and coordinate the right workshop or service engineer.

Where we operate

Our base is Singapore. We serve vessel owners, operators, ship managers and yards across Southeast Asia and India.
